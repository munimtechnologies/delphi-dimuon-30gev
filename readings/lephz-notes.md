## LEPHZ-A (ALEPH 1996, OPAL 1997)

**ALEPH**, "Mass limit for the standard model Higgs boson with the full LEP-1 ALEPH data sample",
Phys. Lett. B 384 (1996) 427, CERN-PPE/96-079 (INSPIRE 420342).
- Data: about 4.5 million hadronic Z decays, 1989-1995.
- H mu mu selection, as summarised by the agent: >= 6 tracks carrying > 10 % of sqrt(s); each
  lepton p > 3 GeV/c, sum > 20 GeV/c, m_ll > 5 GeV/c^2, sum of pT (with respect to the thrust
  axis of the rest of the event) > 15 GeV/c; at least one lepton identified with the strict
  criteria (the second too, unless it is outside the instrumented region); ONE lepton isolated
  (no charged track in a 30 deg half-angle cone, < 1 GeV of neutral energy); recoil mass
  > 40 GeV/c^2; b-tag P_uds < 0.05.
- Candidates: three H mu mu candidates, Higgs (recoil) masses **49.7 +- 0.5, 51.5 +- 0.5 and
  66.9 +- 0.3 GeV/c^2**. Their dilepton masses and years are not listed.
- Efficiency: (48.1 +- 0.5) % at m_H = 60 GeV (61.0 % with the 1992 selection); no table for
  30-60 GeV.

**OPAL**, "Search for neutral Higgs bosons in Z0 decays using the OPAL detector at LEP",
Z. Phys. C 73 (1997) 189, CERN-PPE/96-118 (INSPIRE 423697).
- Data: about 160 pb^-1, "more than 5 million Z0 decays", to the end of 1995.
- mu mu H0 selection, as summarised: both muons pass tight identification; each muon
  5 < p < 30 GeV; momentum sum > 20 GeV (was 25); a recoil-mass cut replaces the earlier 25 GeV
  hadronic-mass cut; sqrt(d1^2 + d2^2) < 50 micrometres; E_vis > 80 GeV; a secondary vertex with
  >= 3 tracks and decay length significance > 3; a forward-detector and silicon-tungsten veto;
  isolation as in Phys. Lett. B 327 (1994) 397. The recoil-mass and isolation cut values are only
  given by reference to that paper (not read).
- Candidate: one, recoil mass **61.2 +- 1.0 GeV**, muon momenta 19.3 and 7.3 GeV; m_mumu not
  stated (the agent derived about 17 GeV; NOTE-higgs-searches.md section 3 derives 16.8 GeV).
- Efficiency eps_mumu: 8.6 % (m_H = 30), 23.1 % (40), 33.6 % (50), 29.4 % (54), 29.9 % (56),
  28.0 % (58), 30.8 % (60).
- Quoted: "the residual background after all selection cuts is 0.17+-0.02 events in the e+e-
  channel and 0.21+-0.03 events in the mu+mu- channel" (for Higgs masses above 50 GeV).

## LEPHZ-B (L3 1996, DELPHI 1994)

**L3**, "Search for neutral Higgs boson production through the process e+ e- -> Z* H0",
Phys. Lett. B 385 (1996) 454, CERN-PPE/96-095 (INSPIRE 421562).
- Data: 3.05 million hadronic Z decays, 1991-1994 (about 114 pb^-1).
- High-mass mu mu H0 selection, as summarised: two muon tracks (distance of closest approach
  < 3.5 sigma); > 4 tracks and >= 8 calorimetric clusters; thrust < 0.92; p1 + p2 > 20 GeV with
  each > 3.4 GeV; isolation D_1, D_2 < 2.5 and D_1 x D_2 < 0.25 for the two muons; the smaller of
  the two BGO energies in a 15 deg cone minus a 3 deg cone < 200 MeV; hadron-calorimeter energy
  in a 30 deg cone minus 3 deg < 4 GeV for each muon and product < 1 GeV^2; no cut on m_mumu or
  on the recoil mass; no neural network.
- Low-mass selection (m_H 2-15 GeV): > 2 and <= 10 tracks, >= 7 clusters, p1 + p2 > 30 GeV,
  max p < 43 GeV, m_mumu > 15 GeV, recoil < 25 GeV, min D < 0.3, min BGO energy < 250 MeV.
- Candidates: **none** in the high-mass mu mu selection. Two low-mass events, recoil 5.3 +- 1.0
  GeV (1992) and 9.6 +- 1.0 GeV (1993); m_mumu not listed.
- Efficiency mu mu H0: 37.0 % (m_H = 30), 37.1 % (40), 36.1 % (50), 32.3 % (60).
- The agent notes that L3 does not give m(mu mu) or m(ee) for its candidates.

**DELPHI**, "Search for the standard model Higgs boson in Z0 decays", Nucl. Phys. B 421 (1994) 3,
CERN-PPE/94-46 (INSPIRE 374451).
- Data: about 1 million hadronic Z decays, 1991-92 (34.6 pb^-1); 935616 used for mu mu.
- mu mu selection, as summarised: >= 6 charged; an opposite-charge pair with p > 5 GeV/c each
  and opening angle > 30 deg; dimuon tag (one muon at any level, the other standard or tight);
  >= 2 recoil jets; missing mass > 15 GeV; recoil visible mass > 10 GeV; p_max > 10 GeV/c;
  isolation from the jets > 40 deg and > 20 deg for the two muons; no m_mumu cut; no neural
  network.
- Candidates (the four low-mass lepton-channel events of the abstract, all 1992): ee, recoil
  15.4; ee, 19.2; ee, 18.9; **mu mu, recoil 27.8 +- 1.9 GeV/c^2**. m_ll is not stated (the
  agent estimated about 35, 51, 10 and 20 GeV, not used here).
- Efficiency mu mu: 54.6 % (m_H = 35), 54.3 % (40), 52.8 % (50), 54.5 % (55), 54.0 % (60);
  30 GeV not given.

## How lephz 0.1.0 reads the open points

- "missing mass > 15 GeV" (DELPHI) is read as the mass recoiling against the muon pair, and
  "recoil visible mass > 10 GeV" as the visible mass of the rest of the event.
- "isolation from the jets > 40 deg and > 20 deg" (DELPHI) is read as the harder muon > 40 deg,
  the other > 20 deg (variants: both > 40 deg).
- The ALEPH 1996 b-tag is applied as a factor 0.8 for events that contain b bbar.
- OPAL's unread recoil and isolation cuts get stand-ins, and OPAL is left out of the nominal
  combination.
